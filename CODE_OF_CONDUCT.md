# Code Of Conduct

KnoArbor follows a simple contributor conduct rule: be respectful, concrete, and
focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and professional language.
- Discuss technical tradeoffs with evidence.
- Respect privacy: do not ask contributors to share secrets, private notes, or
  raw local vaults publicly.
- Keep issues focused on reproducible behavior and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private data, credentials, logs, or local files.
- Repeated off-topic comments that prevent maintainers from resolving issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments that violate this policy. Repeated
or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent blocking from project
spaces.

Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issue
threads.
